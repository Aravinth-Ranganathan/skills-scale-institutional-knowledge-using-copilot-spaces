# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to OctoAcme project management docs. This repository contains our standardized, lightweight process guidance for running cross-functional projects from initiation through close. The materials prioritize customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety so teams can deliver reliably and learn continuously.

OctoAcme runs projects through a staged lifecycle — Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective — supported by compact artifacts (Project One-pager, backlog templates, Risk Register, Definition of Done) and checklists. Initiation validates the business need and success metrics; Planning turns approved initiatives into a prioritized backlog with estimates, acceptance criteria, and a release milestone map; Execution uses a visible project board (Backlog 12 Ready 12 In Progress 12 In Review 12 QA 12 Done), a predictable PR workflow, and CI gating to keep work small and reviewable. Releases follow pre-release checks, smoke tests, and rollback plans, and are followed by post-deploy verification.

Roles are defined to ensure clear ownership: Project Managers coordinate delivery, risk, and communications; Product Managers set vision and success metrics; Developers implement, test, and document; QA validates acceptance criteria. This alignment clarifies handoffs and responsibilities across the lifecycle.

Communication and quality practices are built into the cadence: daily standups for blockers, weekly delivery syncs and status, demos at sprint or milestone end, and monthly stakeholder updates. Blocker escalation is tiered (team 12 PM 12 Product Lead 12 Sponsor) and security incidents follow a dedicated runbook. Continuous improvement is enforced through retrospectives that convert learnings into prioritized action items tracked in the backlog.
