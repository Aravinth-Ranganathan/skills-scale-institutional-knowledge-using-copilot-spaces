# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, requirements, approvals, and resource allocation. They represent customer and business interests, set strategic priorities, and ensure projects align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Provide budget and resource approval
- Participate in project kickoff and major decision gates
- Review and approve release plans and scope changes
- Escalate business-level blockers and trade-offs
- Champion the project across the organization

### Goals
- Maximize business value and ROI
- Ensure strategic alignment with organizational objectives
- Reduce scope creep and timeline risks
- Enable effective resource allocation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Manager on prioritization and vision
- Reviews proposals and timelines with Project Manager
- Provides input on release decisions with engineering leads

### Typical Communication
- Kickoff and decision gate meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based business updates
- Risk and scope change escalations
- Release approval and announcement coordination

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria, identify risks early, and maintain quality thresholds throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and QA strategies aligned with acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with developers on testability and definition of done
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Identify quality risks and blockers
- Validate features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Participate in retrospectives to improve testing processes

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable features
- Minimize defects in production
- Provide early feedback to reduce rework
- Improve test coverage and automation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Reviews acceptance criteria with Product Manager and developers
- Participates in sprint planning and demo reviews
- Escalates quality risks to Project Manager
- Collaborates with DevOps on smoke test and deployment validation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria walkthroughs
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- Release readiness checks and post-deploy verification

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design oversight, and architectural guidance. They mitigate technical risks, ensure scalability, and maintain code and system quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical design and architecture proposals
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure alignment with system design principles and best practices
- Mentor developers and review complex code changes
- Participate in dependency planning and integration coordination
- Guide technology choices and trade-off decisions

### Goals
- Maintain system quality, scalability, and reliability
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable rapid iteration without compromising quality
- Ensure interoperability and integration success

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates with developers on design and code reviews
- Reviews technical proposals during planning
- Escalates technical risks to Project Manager
- Partners with DevOps on deployment and infrastructure concerns

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions
- Technical risk discussions in planning and standups
- Code review comments and mentoring
- Architecture documentation and decision logs

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Delivery Coordinators facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and ensure the team adheres to agreed-upon rituals and workflows. They enable team velocity and maintain process health.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and escalate blockers preventing team progress
- Track velocity and sprint metrics
- Coach team on agile principles and continuous improvement
- Manage project board and workflow visibility
- Support cross-team coordination and dependency management

### Goals
- Maintain team velocity and predictability
- Remove impediments to delivery
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Improve team collaboration and communication

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Facilitates communication between developers, Product Manager, and Project Manager
- Escalates blockers and risks
- Supports retrospective action item tracking
- Coordinates standups and ceremony scheduling

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Sprint and retrospective meeting coordination
- Blocker and impediment tracking
- Metrics and velocity reporting

---

## Communications / Marketing Lead

### Role Summary
Communications and Marketing Leads manage stakeholder messaging, release announcements, and customer-facing documentation. They ensure consistent, clear, and timely communication about project outcomes and releases.

### Responsibilities
- Draft and coordinate release notes and customer announcements
- Manage stakeholder communication timelines and messaging
- Create and update customer-facing documentation
- Coordinate launch messaging and go-to-market activities
- Gather feedback for product improvements
- Ensure messaging aligns with product vision and brand guidelines

### Goals
- Deliver clear, timely communication to all audiences
- Maximize adoption and understanding of new features
- Build customer confidence and satisfaction
- Align all messaging with product and organizational goals

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Manager on feature positioning and messaging
- Works with Project Manager on release timelines and announcements
- Collaborates with developers and QA on technical accuracy of documentation
- Coordinates with Stakeholders on launch strategy

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and announcement coordination
- Draft reviews and stakeholder feedback
- Launch and announcement cadence planning
- Customer feedback and engagement tracking

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, monitoring, and incident response. They enable safe, reliable, and repeatable deployments and maintain system observability.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure, scaling, and reliability
- Coordinate deployments and manage deployment windows
- Set up monitoring, alerting, and observability
- Respond to incidents and support troubleshooting
- Collaborate on rollback strategies and disaster recovery
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, repeatable deployments
- Maintain system reliability and uptime
- Minimize mean time to recovery (MTTR) for incidents
- Reduce manual effort and improve automation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with developers on code quality and deployment readiness
- Collaborates with QA on smoke testing and deployment validation
- Works with Project Manager on deployment scheduling
- Participates in incident response with Sponsor and on-call teams
- Provides infrastructure insights for technical planning

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and coordination
- Infrastructure requirements review
- Incident response and status updates
- Monitoring and performance metrics reporting
- Runbook and documentation updates
